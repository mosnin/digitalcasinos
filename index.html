DIGITAL CASINOS

An open voxel world. Buy land. Build casinos. Run the house.

  • WASD move   Mouse look   Space jump   Shift sprint
  • B buy the parcel you're standing on
  • C construct a casino on your owned parcel
  • E enter casino / sit at a game / use exits
  • G (inside a casino) toggle game-placement mode
  • M world map   Esc release mouse

Tip: you start with coins. Find an empty green parcel and press B.
